Frequently Asked Questions

Not sure which treatment to choose, or what to expect when you visit? Here are the questions I’m asked most often.

Choosing a treatment

I’m not sure which treatment is right for me — where do I start?

A good starting point is how you’d like to feel afterwards:

  • Tense, achy or carrying stress in your shoulders — a Back, Neck & Shoulders Massage (30 minutes) or a Full Body Massage (45 or 60 minutes).
  • In need of a proper switch-off — the Luxury Massage, or one of the longer experiences in the Zen Collection.
  • Struggling to sleep or quieten your mind — the Maple Garden Sleep Well Experience, Mindfulness Meditation & Massage, or 1:1 Meditation.
  • Wanting brighter, smoother skin — one of the facials, from a 30-minute Express Facial to Dermaplaning or a Chemical Peel Facial.
  • Feeling puffy or sluggish — Facial or Full Body Lymphatic Drainage.

If you’re still undecided, get in touch and I’ll help you choose.

What’s the difference between the massage treatments?
  • Full Body Massage (45 or 60 minutes) — a relaxing full body massage that also includes face, chest and scalp.
  • Luxury Massage (75 minutes) — a full head-to-toe massage with extras: a dry body brush to start, a back scrub, hot & cold stone therapy and Indian head massage.
  • Hot Stone Massage (30 or 75 minutes) — warm basalt stones are used to ease tension and melt away stress, either full body or on the back, neck and shoulders.
  • Back, Neck & Shoulders Massage (30 minutes) — targeted work on the areas where most of us hold the most tension.
  • Indian Head Massage (30 minutes) — head, chest and face massage, with an optional colour visualisation meditation.

Prices and full descriptions are on the Treatments & Services page.

What is the Zen Collection?

The Zen Collection is my set of longer, combined treatments — ideal when you want a real treat, or can’t choose between a massage and a facial.

  • The Zen Experience (90 minutes) — body massage, back scrub, breathwork, hot & cold stone therapy, Indian head massage and a facial cleanse and exfoliation.
  • The Dreamy Head Experience (60 minutes) — all about your face, head and hair: cleanse, exfoliation, mask, facial massage with basalt and marine stones, Indian head massage and finishing oils.
  • Reset & Restore (75–90 minutes) — build your own: choose an Indian Head Massage, Express Facial, Hopi Ears or Back Massage & Exfoliation, paired with either a Maple Glow Facial or a Body Massage.
What is lymphatic drainage?

Lymphatic drainage is a gentle, flowing treatment that uses slow, rhythmic movements to encourage lymphatic circulation and support the body’s natural fluid balance. It is much lighter than a traditional massage, and deeply relaxing.

  • Full Body Lymphatic Drainage (70 minutes) — designed to leave you feeling lighter and refreshed.
  • Facial Lymphatic Drainage (40 minutes) — helps reduce puffiness, define facial contours and ease facial tension, for a fresher, more sculpted appearance.
Can I have a treatment while I’m pregnant?

Yes. Pregnancy Massage is available for pregnancies over 12 weeks, with approval from your healthcare provider, and is designed to relieve the common aches and pains of pregnancy. The Dreamy Head Experience is also suitable for pregnancies over 12 weeks.

If you have another treatment in mind, please let me know you’re pregnant when you get in touch and I’ll advise on what’s suitable.

What is Hopi ear candling?

Hopi ear candling is a calming treatment in which a hollow candle is gently placed at the entrance to the ear and lit while you lie comfortably on your side. Clients choose it to help manage ear wax and to relieve migraines and sinus pain. At Maple Garden it is finished with a lymphatic drainage massage to the face and sinus area, and the whole treatment takes 45 minutes.

Facials & skincare

Which facial should I choose?
  • Express Facial (30 minutes) — a quick refresh: cleanse, mask, facial massage, serum and moisturise.
  • Maple Garden Glow Facial (50 minutes) — a bespoke facial that begins with a skin consultation and includes a shoulder and chest massage while your mask works. It can be upgraded to Professional Results to target specific skin concerns.
  • Dermaplaning (70 minutes) — exfoliation that removes fine facial hair and leaves skin brighter and smoother.
  • Chemical Peel Facial (45 minutes) — a professional MediMD peel targeting dullness, fine lines and uneven texture.
  • Facial Lymphatic Drainage (40 minutes) — gentle, rhythmic massage to reduce puffiness and give a natural lifted look.

See the facials menu for prices, or ask me if you’d like a recommendation for your skin.

Is there any downtime after a chemical peel?

No — the Chemical Peel Facial uses professional MediMD products with no downtime, so you can carry on with your day. Results build gradually and are at their best around 4–7 days after your treatment.

Which products do you use?

Facials use professional skincare from Eve Taylor London and MediMD, chosen to suit your skin. Massage treatments use Eve Taylor London aromatherapy oils.

Meditation

How does 1:1 Meditation work?

1:1 Meditation is a bespoke course built around you. It starts with a planning consultation, followed by two one-hour sessions, and can focus on anxiety, stress, sleep or general mindfulness. Sessions can be in person in the garden room or online.

Can I combine meditation with a massage?

Yes — meditation is woven into many of my treatments. Mindfulness Meditation & Massage combines a full body massage with mindfulness meditation and breathwork, the Maple Garden Sleep Well Experience is an evening massage with a body scan meditation to help you wind down, and the Indian Head Massage can include a colour visualisation meditation.
